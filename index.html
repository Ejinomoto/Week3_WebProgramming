Arcane

Arcane is a 6★ Nature Caster Operator from Hongshan Academy of Sciences in Arknights: Endfield. She is an Arts Unit user with switchable skill types and the ability to apply long-lasting Corrosion. When she has more INT, she can deal massive DMG. When she has more WILL, her combo skill can apply Arts Infliction of any element
